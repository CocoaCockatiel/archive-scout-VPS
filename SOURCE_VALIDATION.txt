Archive Scout 1.0.1 source validation
Date: 2026-08-13

Audit basis
- P1 interrupted-index resume: fixed. Resume re-enters the saved CDX queue before download work.
- P1 shared-VPS throttling: fixed in the official process. Concurrent projects now share request-start pacing and one Wayback host gate; HTTP 503 coordinates even without Retry-After.
- P2 visible counter reset: fixed. Normal resume progress is cumulative from persisted capture states.
- P2 safe pauses reported as failures by Discord: the Discord fork is not part of the official repository. Archive Scout 1.0.1 now persists operation progress/status and exposes is_recoverable_pause() so wrappers can classify saved pauses without treating them as crashes.

Network/recovery validation
- Headerless HTTP 503 enters the coordinated Wayback host pause and can defer safely after the configured budget.
- Shared request pacing coordinates independent clients in the same process.
- Shared host-gate identity is process-wide and adopts conservative pause settings.
- Legacy zero pause budgets normalize to finite safety defaults: 15 minutes and 8 coordinated incidents.
- Resume invokes index_archive before download_archive, allowing the exact persisted CDX queue to continue.
- Recoverable ConnectivityPaused and RateLimitDeferred states are distinguishable through is_recoverable_pause().

Progress validation
- Cumulative progress is derived from persisted capture states plus the remaining disk-backed queue.
- operation_runs.progress_json is updated from owner-thread progress events for external integrations.
- Existing 1.0 AI relevance, external embedded-media, scanning, reports, recovery, and analysis tests remain green.
