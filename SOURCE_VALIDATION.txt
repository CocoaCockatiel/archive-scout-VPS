External embedded-media validation
- Modern lazy image attributes, srcset, video/source/poster tags, social metadata, preload hints, CSS/background URLs, legacy player configuration, and Wayback replay URL unwrapping are covered.
- External-media discovery is persistent by source-document content hash, so unchanged pages are not reparsed on repeat runs.
- Exact external-media CDX lookups use a persistent queue and bounded concurrency.
- The dedicated external-media workflow defines external hosts relative to the site actually indexed and scanned, not optional direct-media targets.
- Known Wayback-excluded or robots-blocked hosts are short-circuited instead of repeatedly queried.
- Media downloads retain direct-to-disk streaming and incremental hashing.

Site issue validation
- Wayback exclusion, robots restrictions, missing captures, invalid replay responses, access restrictions, rate limits, timeouts, connection failures, TLS failures, and server errors have distinct classifications where evidence permits.
- Repeated host/stage issues are deduplicated with occurrence counts.
- Confirmed exclusion/robots conditions can act as host-level circuits while unrelated targets continue.
